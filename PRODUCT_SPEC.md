# Product specification — steady. MVP

## User promise

A movement practice that meets you where you are. Three actions: check in, get a realistic workout, show up and track it.

## Primary users

Adults seeking consistency more than performance. Secondary: frequent travelers and busy multi-sport amateurs.

## Product hypothesis

People are more likely to return to movement when they can select the time available, disclose energy/soreness, and receive a short plan that is achievable without equipment.

## Product boundaries

- General wellness, not diagnosis, rehabilitation, or personal medical coaching
- Not for children
- No calorie, body-weight, or body-shape scoring
- No streak shame, punitive notifications, or forced daily use
- No credential collection, sensor/GPS recording, or account sign-up

## Core journey

1. Onboard once. Name optional; select primary goal, baseline experience, equipment, and days-per-week target.
2. Daily readiness check: energy, mood, soreness, injury/pain flag, time budget, desired modality.
3. Preview and start a deterministic plan with timed warmup, main session, recovery.
4. Follow cues, pause, skip, restart, exit; save partial activity separately from completed workouts.
5. Completed workouts count towards weekly movement days and streak when at least 70% of the timed session was actually performed; partial saves never count. Adjust preferences any time.

## Rules and safeguards

- Reported pain/injury → mindfulness-only, without exercise instructions.
- High soreness or low energy → mobility-focused gentle session, with stop-if-pain guidance.
- Beginner level → beginner exercises only.
- No dumbbells selected → never assign dumbbell exercises.
- Every generated session duration equals the chosen time budget exactly.
- Movement plans contain warmup and cooldown; mindfulness-only does not need either.
- Weekly target counts distinct days rather than raw session count. Week starts Monday.

## Metrics for prototype pilot

Activation: first session started and completed. Retention: proportion of testers completing at least one session in week two. Engagement: median weekly completed sessions, completion rate, and total movement days. Qualitative: did the app reduce decision friction?

## Deferred

Audio guidance, exercise video, progressive training blocks, running GPS, Apple Health / Health Connect, AI plan generation, account sync, leaderboards, paid subscriptions, workout sharing, push reminders.
